View Hidden Photo Metadata (EXIF), Free

This free image metadata and EXIF viewer reads the technical details embedded inside a photo by the camera or phone that took it — camera make and model, exposure settings like aperture, shutter speed and ISO, the date and time the photo was taken, image orientation, and GPS location if it was recorded. It works as an online EXIF data reader and photo metadata inspector, with a search box to quickly filter to the fields you care about.

Use it to check when and where a photo was really taken, review camera settings from a shoot, verify whether a photo still has GPS data before sharing it publicly, or inspect a file's technical details. Everything is parsed locally in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The photo's metadata is read entirely in your browser using JavaScript, and the image itself is never uploaded anywhere.

Why does my photo show no metadata?

Many apps and social media platforms strip EXIF data when you save or share a photo, so no metadata being found is common and does not mean the tool failed.

Can this tool read GPS location from a photo?

Yes, if the photo still has GPS data embedded. It will be shown as coordinates along with a link to view the location on a map.

Does this work on PNG or WebP images?

EXIF metadata is a JPEG feature. PNG and WebP files generally do not contain EXIF data, so this tool works best with JPEG photos straight from a camera or phone.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no limit, since all processing happens on your own device.
