    Online Video Merger

    This online video merger joins two or more video clips into a single video in your browser. Add your clips, put them in the right order, trim each one, choose the output size and fit mode, and download one finished video.

    It is useful for combining phone clips, joining screen recordings, building highlight reels and stitching together story or reel segments. Your videos are never uploaded, so they stay on your device.

    How to Merge Videos

    1. 1

      Add Your Videos

      Select or drag in two or more video files. You can add up to 20 clips.

    2. 2

      Arrange and Set Options

      Reorder clips with the arrows, trim each clip, then choose output size, fit mode, quality, frame rate, fades and audio.

    3. 3

      Merge and Download

      Select Merge Videos, wait for it to finish, check the preview, then download your merged video.

    Video Merging Uses

    Clips with different sizes are handled automatically. Fit adds bars so nothing is cropped, Fill crops to cover the frame, and Stretch resizes to the exact output size. Choose a vertical or square size for social media.

    Merging runs at normal playback speed in your browser, so the time needed is about the total length of your clips. Output is MP4 or WebM depending on browser support. WebM files made in a browser may not show their total length in some players; they still play normally.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a video merger?

    A video merger joins several video clips into one continuous video. This free online video merger works directly in your browser.

    How do I merge videos online?

    Add two or more videos, arrange them in order, choose your settings, select Merge Videos, then download the merged video.

    Is my video uploaded to a server?

    No. All merging happens on your device in your browser, so your videos are never uploaded.

    Can I merge videos of different sizes?

    Yes. Choose an output size and a fit mode. Fit adds bars, Fill crops to cover, and Stretch resizes the clip to the output size.

    How many videos can I merge?

    You can merge up to 20 videos in one go.

    Can I change the order of the clips?

    Yes. Use the up and down arrows on each clip to change the order before you merge.

    Can I trim each clip?

    Yes. Every clip has start and end fields in seconds so you can use only the part you need.

    Why does merging take as long as the video?

    The video is re-encoded in real time in your browser. Keep the tab open and visible until it finishes.

    Is this video merger free?

    Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up and adds no watermark.
