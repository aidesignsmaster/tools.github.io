    Join Video Clips Into One Video

    Video Merger puts two or more clips together in the order you choose. Trim the start and end of each clip, duplicate a clip, pick one output size for the whole video, and decide whether clips are fitted with bars, cropped to fill the frame or stretched. You can also add a short fade for every clip, remove the audio, and choose quality, frame rate and format. Everything happens in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

    The clips are played one after another and recorded as a new video, so the time it takes equals the total length of your clips. For long projects, desktop editing software will be faster.

    How to Use It

    1. Select or drop two or more videos. Each one appears in a list with a picture, length and size.
    2. Drag rows or use the arrows to set the order, and trim any clip with its start and end seconds.
    3. Choose the output size, fit mode, quality, frame rate and format.
    4. Press Merge Videos, keep the tab open until it finishes, then preview and download.

    Practical Tips

    Match the size to the destination. Use 1080 × 1920 for vertical stories and reels, 1080 × 1080 for square posts and 1920 × 1080 for widescreen. Pick Fit if you never want anything cropped, and change the bar colour to match your design. Use Fill to avoid bars when clips have different shapes. Trim first so you do not spend time recording parts you will not use. Stay on this tab while merging, because browsers slow down background tabs.

    When It Helps

    Use it to join phone clips from one event, combine screen recordings into one lesson, build a short highlight reel, put an intro and outro around a main video, or prepare a single file to send instead of many.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is my video uploaded to a server?

    No. Your clips are joined inside your browser, so they never leave your device. No sign-up is needed and the merged video has no watermark.

    How many videos can I merge, and in what order?

    You can merge up to 20 clips. Drag the rows or use the arrows to set the order, sort them by name, reverse the list, or duplicate a clip to use it more than once. Each clip can be trimmed with its own start and end times.

    Can I merge videos with different sizes?

    Yes. Choose one output size and a fit mode. Fit keeps the whole picture and adds bars in the colour you pick, Fill covers the frame and crops the edges, and Stretch resizes the clip to the exact output size.

    Why does merging take as long as the videos?

    Browsers cannot join most video formats directly, so the clips are played one after another and recorded as a new video in real time. Ten minutes of clips take about ten minutes. Keep the tab open and in front until it finishes.

    Which formats can I use?

    You can add any video your browser can play, usually MP4, WebM and MOV with common codecs. The merged file is saved as MP4 when your browser can record H.264, otherwise as WebM. The format menu shows what your browser supports.

    Will the quality drop?

    Because the clips are re-encoded there can be a small difference from the originals. Choose High quality, use an output size that matches your clips, and avoid a lower frame rate than your source to keep the result close to the original.
