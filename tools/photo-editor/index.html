Edit Your Photo Right in Your Browser

This free photo editor covers the jobs people do every day: crop a picture to the right shape, put a caption or a name on it, add a fun emoji or an arrow, hide a face or a number plate, and make the colors look better. There is nothing to install and no account to make. Pick a photo, edit it, and download the result.

How to Use the Photo Editor

  1. Tap Choose Photo and pick a picture from your phone or computer. You can also drop or paste one.
  2. Pick a tool: Text, Emoji, Shapes or Draw to decorate, Blur to hide private details, Colors to improve the look, or Crop to cut, rotate and add a border.
  3. Drag items to move them. On a phone, use two fingers to make them bigger or to turn them. Tap Undo if you change your mind.
  4. Choose JPG or PNG and tap Download to save your edited photo.

Tips for Hiding Faces and Private Details

Use the Blur tool before you share a photo with a car number plate, an ID card, an address or a child's face. Pixelate hides details strongly and is the safer choice for numbers and text. Draw a circle around the part you want to hide, or paint over it. The hidden part is changed in the picture itself, so it cannot be brought back from the saved file, and you can still tap Undo before you download.

Tips for Cropping and Colors

Pick a crop shape such as 1:1 for a profile picture, 4:5 for a feed post or 9:16 for a story, then drag the box over the part you want. For colors, try Auto Enhance first, then fine-tune with Brightness, Contrast and Shadows. A little Sharpen helps soft photos, and a light Vignette draws the eye to the middle.

Tips for Text and Emoji

Use a light text color with a dark outline so the words are easy to read on any photo. Pinch with two fingers on a phone to make an item bigger or to turn it. The fonts come from your device, so they can look a little different on a phone, a Mac and a Windows computer. The emoji are drawn by your device too, so they match the style you already see in your chats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this photo editor free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and has no watermark on your photo.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens in your browser on your own device. Your photo is never uploaded or stored.

How do I crop a photo?

Open the Crop tool, pick a shape such as 1:1 or 4:5 or leave it on Free, drag the box or its handles over the part you want, and tap Apply Crop. Tap Undo to go back.

How do I blur a face or a number plate?

Open the Blur tool, choose Pixelate or Blur, and paint over the part you want to hide or draw a circle around it. Pixelate hides details more strongly.

Can I get the hidden part back after I blur it?

Not from the saved file. The blur changes the picture itself. You can tap Undo before you download, and it is a good idea to keep your original photo.

How do I add text to a photo?

Open the Text tool, type your words, and tap Add Text to Photo. Then drag the text where you want it and change the font, color, size and outline.

How do I add an arrow or a shape?

Open the Shapes tool and tap an arrow, box, circle, star, heart or highlight. Drag it into place and use the Size, Rotate and color controls.

How do I delete text, an emoji or a shape?

Tap it on the photo to select it, then tap the red x on its corner, or tap the Delete button next to Undo. If you delete something by mistake, tap Undo.

How do I make a sticker bigger or turn it?

On a phone, put two fingers on the screen and pinch to resize and twist to turn. You can also use the Size and Rotate sliders under the photo.

Will my text, drawing and emoji be saved in the photo?

Yes. When you tap Download, the text, shapes, drawing and emoji are drawn into the picture, together with your color changes.

Which file types can I open and save?

You can open JPG, PNG and WebP photos and save the result as JPG or PNG.

Does it change the size of my photo?

Only if you crop it or add a border. Photos larger than 4000 pixels on the long side are scaled down when they are opened, so the editor stays fast on phones.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is made for phones as well as computers. You can paint, draw, drag and pinch with your fingers.
