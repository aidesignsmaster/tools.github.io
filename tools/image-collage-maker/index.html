Make a Photo Collage Online for Free

This free image collage maker combines up to 12 photos into a single picture. Pick a layout such as a classic grid, one big photo with smaller ones beside it, a single row or a single column, then adjust spacing, rounded corners, background color and canvas size. The preview updates instantly, so you can see exactly what you will download.

It is made for Instagram posts and stories, Facebook and WhatsApp status, product photo sheets, family albums, travel memories and before-and-after comparisons. Your photos are processed locally in your browser with the Canvas API, so nothing is uploaded and there is no watermark.

How to Make a Collage

  1. Select or drop your photos (up to 12, JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. Choose a layout and canvas size, such as Square or Story.
  3. Set spacing, rounded corners and a background color. Use the arrows to reorder photos or the cross to remove one.
  4. Download your collage as PNG or JPG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The collage is built locally in your browser using the Canvas API, so your photos never leave your device.

How many photos can I add?

You can combine up to 12 photos in one collage. You can add more at any time until you reach the limit.

Which image formats are supported?

You can add JPG, PNG and WebP photos and download the collage as a PNG or a JPG.

Can I change the order of my photos?

Yes. Use the arrow buttons under each thumbnail to move a photo, or press Shuffle Photos to mix them randomly.

What size is the finished collage?

You choose the size: square 1080x1080, portrait 1080x1350, story 1080x1920, landscape 1920x1080 or A4 print 1240x1754 pixels.

Is the collage maker free and watermark-free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no sign-up and adds no watermark to your collage.
