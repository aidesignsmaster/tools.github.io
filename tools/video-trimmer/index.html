Cut, Trim and Shorten Videos in Your Browser

Video Trimmer & Cutter saves just the part of a video that you need. Drag the two markers or type exact times, preview the selection, and the tool records that section into a new file. You can keep the selected part or remove it and join the rest, change the speed, mute the sound, add a fade, and choose the quality and size of the result. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

The tool plays your clip and records it again, so the length of the work equals the length of the result. It is best for short and medium clips; very long videos are better cut with desktop software.

How to Use It

  1. Select or drop a video. It opens in the preview with a trim bar underneath.
  2. Drag the start and end markers, or type times, or press Start = Now and End = Now while the video plays.
  3. Choose to keep or remove the selected part, then set format, quality, size and speed.
  4. Press Cut Video, wait for the progress bar to finish, check the preview and download.

Practical Tips

Use the frame buttons to land exactly on the moment you want. Keep this tab in front while cutting, because browsers slow down hidden tabs. Click the page once before cutting if your browser asks for permission to play sound. Choose High quality for the closest match to the original, or Small file for sharing in chat apps. For social media, trim to the exact length first, then use the Video Compressor if the file is still large.

When It Helps

Use it to take a highlight out of a longer recording, remove a mistake from the middle of a screen recording, shorten a clip for a story or reel, speed up a slow section, or save a short piece of a lecture or meeting you recorded yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The video is played and re-recorded inside your browser, so it never leaves your device. No account or sign-up is needed, and the result has no watermark.

Why does cutting take as long as the clip?

Browsers cannot cut most video formats directly, so the tool plays the part you chose and records it again in real time. A 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds, and a clip sped up to 2× takes half that. Keep the tab open and visible until it finishes.

Which formats can I open and save?

You can open any video your browser can play, usually MP4, WebM and MOV with common codecs. The result is saved as MP4 when your browser can record H.264, otherwise as WebM. The format menu shows only what your browser supports.

Will the quality drop?

Because the clip is re-encoded, there can be a small quality or size difference from the original. For the best result choose High quality, keep the original resolution and avoid very low frame rates.

Can I remove a section from the middle?

Yes. Choose Remove the selected part and the tool keeps everything before the start marker and after the end marker, joined together. You can also change speed, mute the audio or add a short fade in and out.

Why is the sound missing or the video choppy?

Some browsers block sound until you click the page, and most browsers slow down background tabs. Click once on the page before cutting and keep this tab in front. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari are supported, with Chrome and Edge usually the smoothest.
