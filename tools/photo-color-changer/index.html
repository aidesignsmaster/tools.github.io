Change the Color of Anything in a Photo

This free photo color changer lets you click on any object in a picture — a shirt, a dress, a car, a wall, a flower — and swap its color for a new one while keeping the original shading, shadows and fabric folds intact. It works as an online color replacement tool and a selective recolor filter, finding every pixel with a similar color to the one you clicked and blending in the new color smoothly at the edges.

Use it to preview a dress in a different color, change a car's paint color, try out a new wall color for a room, or restyle a product photo. Everything runs locally in your browser using the Canvas API, so your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool know which object to recolor?

Click anywhere on the photo to pick a color. The tool then finds every pixel with a similar color, based on the tolerance you set, and replaces it while keeping the original shading and texture.

Will it only change the object I clicked on?

It changes every area of a similar color, not just the exact spot you clicked. If another object shares that color, it may change too — lower the tolerance for a more precise selection.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The recoloring happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API, and your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Will the shading and folds in clothing still look natural?

Yes. The tool replaces the hue and saturation of matched pixels but keeps their original brightness, so shadows, highlights and fabric folds are preserved.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark, since all processing happens on your own device.
