Pick a Color From an Image

Image Color Picker lets you upload photos, screenshots, graphics, logos and other images, then click directly on the image to identify colors. The tool shows HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK values and also creates a quick palette of common image colors. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pick a color from an image?

Select an image, then click any point on the displayed image. The selected color and its HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK values will update instantly.

What is the Image Color Palette?

It is a quick set of common colors detected from your uploaded image. You can click a palette color to view and copy its color codes.

Where are my picked colors saved?

Picked colors are kept only in the current page session in your browser. They are not uploaded to a server.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Image processing and color picking happen locally in your browser. Your image does not need to be sent to a server.
