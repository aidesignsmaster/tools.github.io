Sharpen and Upscale Photos Free, in Your Browser

This free image sharpener and photo upscaler increases the size of a picture with smooth resampling, then applies an unsharp-mask sharpening filter to bring out edges and fine detail. It works as a browser-based photo enhancer, an online image upscaler, and a picture sharpener in one, with no upload, no sign-up, and no watermark, because every pixel is processed locally on your own device using the Canvas API.

Use it to sharpen a slightly blurry photo, upscale a small image before printing, boost clarity on a product photo, or clean up a screenshot. This tool uses classic image-processing filters rather than a neural network, so it is instant and completely free, though it enhances existing detail rather than inventing new detail the way a heavier AI model might.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real AI tool?

No. This tool uses classic image-processing techniques — smooth resampling for upscaling and an unsharp-mask filter for sharpening — not a neural network. It runs instantly and for free because of that.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Every step happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photo never leaves your device.

Why does my image look pixelated even after upscaling?

Upscaling makes an image bigger and smoother, but it cannot invent detail that was never captured in the original photo. Very low-resolution or heavily compressed images will still show some softness.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up, no watermark, and no limit, since all processing happens on your own device.

What is the maximum image size supported?

Very large images can be slow to process in the browser, so this tool works best with photos up to about 12 megapixels before upscaling.
