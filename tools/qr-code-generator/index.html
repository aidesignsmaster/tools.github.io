Create Custom QR Codes for Free

This free QR code generator turns any text, website link, phone number or short message into a scannable QR code, right in your browser. Customize the foreground and background colors, choose a size up to 1024px, and pick an error-correction level to control how much damage or overlay the code can tolerate while still scanning correctly. You can also add your own logo to the center of the code for a branded look.

Use it to create a QR code for a business card, a menu, a Wi-Fi network, a social media profile link, or event signage. Every QR code is generated locally in your browser, so nothing you type is ever uploaded, and the code itself never expires since your data is encoded directly into it rather than pointing to a redirect service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. The QR code is generated locally in your browser, and the text or link you enter is never sent anywhere.

Do QR codes expire?

No. A QR code generated here encodes your data directly and does not expire or depend on this website staying online.

Why did my QR code stop scanning after I added a logo?

A logo covers part of the code, so use a high error-correction level (Q or H) when adding one, and keep the logo small relative to the code.

What can I put into a QR code?

Website links, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses, or Wi-Fi details typed in the correct format are all common uses.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no limit, since all processing happens on your own device.
