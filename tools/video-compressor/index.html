Reduce Video Size Without Losing the Picture

Video Compressor makes MP4, WebM and MOV files smaller so they are easier to email, message, upload and store. Choose a ready-made level, type an exact target such as 16 MB, or fine-tune the resolution, frame rate and bitrate yourself. You can also trim the clip, remove the audio and see the new size, the percentage saved and a preview before you download. Your video is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

The clip is played and recorded again at the settings you choose, so it takes about as long as the video itself. Short and medium videos suit this tool best.

How to Use It

  1. Select or drop a video. Its size, length and resolution appear next to the preview.
  2. Choose a compression level, or pick Exact target size and a limit such as 16 MB.
  3. Adjust resolution, frame rate, audio and trim if you wish. The estimate updates as you change things.
  4. Press Compress Video, wait for the progress bar, compare the result and download.

Practical Tips

Lower the resolution first. Going from 1080p to 720p often saves more than any other setting with little visible loss on a phone screen. Remove the audio when you do not need it, which frees bitrate for the picture. Trim the clip to the part you need before compressing, since both time and size drop. Use target mode when a website or app gives a strict size limit. Keep this tab in front while it works, because browsers slow down hidden tabs.

When It Helps

Use it when a video is too big to email, when a chat app or form rejects a large upload, when you want to save space on a drive, or when you need a lighter copy of a screen recording or phone clip to share quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The video is compressed inside your browser, so it never leaves your device. No sign-up is needed and the result has no watermark.

How do I reach an exact file size?

Choose Exact target size in the Compression level menu, pick a size such as 16 MB or type your own in KB, and keep Auto-retry ticked. The tool chooses the resolution and bitrate, and if the first result is too big it tries again with a lower bitrate, up to four times. Very small targets work best with a short clip and the audio removed.

Why does compression take as long as the video?

Browsers cannot shrink most video formats directly, so the clip is played and recorded again in real time. A two-minute video takes about two minutes. Keep the tab open and in front until it finishes, because background tabs are slowed down.

Which formats can I use?

You can open any video your browser can play, usually MP4, WebM and MOV with common codecs. The result is saved as MP4 when your browser can record H.264, otherwise as WebM. The format menu shows only what your browser supports.

Will the quality drop?

Compression always trades some quality for size. Recommended keeps video clear for sharing. Lowering the resolution, frame rate or bitrate makes the file smaller but softer, so compare the preview with the original before you download.

Why is my compressed video not smaller?

If the original is already heavily compressed, re-encoding at a similar bitrate cannot make it much smaller. Choose Small file or Extreme, lower the resolution to 720p or 480p, reduce the frame rate or remove the audio.
