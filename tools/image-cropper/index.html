Free Online Image Cropper

Working Tools Box Image Cropper lets you crop JPG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser as a fast, free image cropping tool. Use this online photo cropper and picture cropper to trim, cut, and resize the visible area of your image without installing any software. Choose a popular aspect ratio like 1:1, 4:3, or 16:9, enter a custom crop ratio, or use Free Crop for flexible cropping. Because it works as a browser-based image cropper, every crop happens on your own device, so this private image cropper never uploads your pictures to a server.

This tool also works as a bulk image cropper and batch photo cropper — select up to 20 images and crop them one at a time or use Crop All Images to apply the same crop settings across your whole batch. Use it to crop a photo for social media, crop an image to square, crop a picture for a profile photo, or prepare a thumbnail with exact dimensions.

How to Crop an Image

  1. Select one or more images.
  2. Choose an aspect ratio or Free Crop.
  3. Move and resize the crop area on the image.
  4. Choose the output format and quality.
  5. Crop the current image or crop all images.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image cropper free?

Yes. The tool is free to use in your browser with no sign-up required.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP images are supported for both upload and output.

Can I crop multiple images?

Yes. You can select up to 20 images and crop them individually or use the crop-all option.

Can I crop an image to a specific aspect ratio?

Yes. Choose a popular ratio like 1:1, 16:9 or 4:5, enter a custom ratio, or use Free Crop for full control.

Will cropping reduce my image quality?

You control the output quality with the quality slider, so you can balance file size against image clarity.
