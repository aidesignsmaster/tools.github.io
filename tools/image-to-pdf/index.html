    Combine Pictures Into a Clean PDF

    Image to PDF Converter puts photos, scans and screenshots into a single PDF, or into separate PDFs if you prefer. You choose the page size, orientation, margins and background, rotate any image, place two, four or eight images on one page, add page numbers, and protect the file with a password. The PDF is built entirely in your browser, so your pictures stay private.

    It is a simple way to hand in homework, send a set of receipts, assemble a portfolio or turn phone photos of a document into one file that is easy to email.

    How to Use It

    1. Select or drop your images. Use the arrows, drag handles or Sort buttons to set the page order, and rotate any sideways pictures.
    2. Pick a page size, orientation and margin. Leave Auto orientation on if your images mix portrait and landscape.
    3. Choose the image quality and any extras such as page numbers or a password.
    4. Press Create PDF, then download it or preview it in a new tab.

    Practical Tips

    Pick the right quality. Original keeps JPG files exactly as they are, which gives the best fidelity but the largest PDF. High suits most photos. Balanced and Small shrink big phone photos so the PDF is easier to email. Choose fit or fill. Fit inside never crops anything; Fill the space covers the page and trims the edges. Use Fit to image when you want each page to match its picture instead of a paper size. Keep margins for printing. Many printers cannot print to the very edge, so a small or medium margin is safer. Remember your password. It cannot be recovered if you forget it.

    When It Helps

    Use it when a form asks for a single PDF upload, when you have photographed a multi-page document with your phone, when you want a printable contact sheet of several pictures, or when you need to send images in a format that looks the same on every device.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I turn images into a PDF?

    Add your pictures, arrange them in the order you want, choose a page size and margin, then press Create PDF. When the file is ready, press Download PDF. Everything happens in your browser, so no upload is needed.

    Can I protect the PDF with a password?

    Yes. Tick Add password, type a password of up to 32 standard characters, and the PDF will ask for it when opened. It uses the standard PDF password protection with 128-bit RC4, which keeps casual readers out but is older than modern AES encryption, so use a dedicated security tool for highly sensitive documents. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.

    Will my images lose quality?

    Choose Original and JPG files that the PDF format can hold directly are embedded exactly as they are, without re-compression. PNG, WebP and other formats are converted to JPEG at the quality you choose, and transparent areas are filled with the background colour. Balanced and Small also reduce very large pictures to keep the PDF light.

    Which files and how many can I add?

    You can add up to 50 images at once, each up to 50 MB. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP work in all common browsers, and AVIF works where your browser can open it. HEIC photos are not supported by browsers, so convert them to JPG first. For animated GIFs only the first frame is used.

    Are my pictures uploaded anywhere?

    No. The PDF is built on your own device with JavaScript, and the page does not send your files to a server. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded, except that the optional ZIP helper for separate PDFs needs to have loaded first.

    What does Pages per sheet do?

    It places two, four or eight images on each page in a neat grid, which is handy for contact sheets, handouts or saving paper when printing. It needs a fixed page size such as A4 and the Merge into one PDF option.
