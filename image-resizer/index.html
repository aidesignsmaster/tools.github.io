Resize Images Online

This free image resizer lets you change the width and height of JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images directly in your browser. Lock the aspect ratio to prevent unwanted stretching, use common preset dimensions, choose an output format, adjust quality, and download the resized result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image?

Upload an image, enter the width and height you need, choose whether to lock the aspect ratio, then click Resize & Download Image.

Can I resize an image to an exact size?

Yes. Enter the exact width and height in pixels. If aspect ratio locking is enabled, changing one dimension automatically adjusts the other.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images. The resized result can be exported as JPG, PNG or WebP.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image resizing happens locally in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.
