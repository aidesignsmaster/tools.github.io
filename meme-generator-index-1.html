Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The meme is created locally in your browser using the Canvas API, and your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I use my own photo instead of a template?

Yes. Upload any JPG, PNG or WebP image and add your own top and bottom text to it.

Can I change the font size and color?

Yes. Use the size slider and color picker to adjust the caption text, and toggle the outline for the classic bold white-on-black meme look.

What format is the meme downloaded as?

Your meme downloads as a PNG image, ready to share anywhere.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark, since all processing happens on your own device.
