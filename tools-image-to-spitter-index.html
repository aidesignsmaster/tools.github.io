Split a Photo Into an Instagram Grid, Free

This free image grid splitter divides a single photo into 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 or 21 equal pieces arranged in a 3-column layout, matching the standard Instagram profile grid. It works as an Instagram grid maker, a puzzle feed splitter, and a photo divider in one, so you can post one large image across several grid slots and have it appear as a single seamless picture on your profile.

Use it to create a panoramic-style grid post, split a group photo across multiple posts, or design a themed 3x3 or 3x6 feed layout. Every piece is generated locally in your browser using the Canvas API, then bundled into a ZIP file for a single, easy download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The image is split locally in your browser using the Canvas API, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How does the Instagram grid layout work?

Each option splits the photo into 3 equal columns and a number of rows (for example, 9 pieces is a 3x3 grid). Posting the pieces to Instagram in the matching order recreates the full photo across your grid.

In what order should I post the pieces?

Pieces are numbered left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Post the highest-numbered piece first and the lowest-numbered piece last, since new posts push older ones down on a profile grid.

Can I download all the pieces at once?

Yes. Use the Download All as ZIP button to get every piece in a single file, or download individual pieces one at a time.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark, since all processing happens on your own device.
