Generate a Favicon From Any Image, Free

This free favicon generator turns an uploaded logo or image into a complete set of standard favicon sizes — 16x16, 32x32, 48x48, an Apple touch icon at 180x180, and a 512x512 PNG for modern browsers and app icons — then bundles them into a single ZIP along with ready-to-paste HTML link tags. It works as an online favicon maker and icon generator, with everything resized directly in your browser.

Use it to create a favicon for a new website, refresh an old site's browser tab icon, or generate app-icon-sized PNGs from a logo. Non-square images are automatically center-cropped to a square first, so any logo works without extra editing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Every favicon size is generated locally in your browser using the Canvas API, and your image is never uploaded anywhere.

What sizes do I get?

16x16, 32x32, 48x48, 180x180 (Apple touch icon) and 512x512 PNG files, bundled together in one ZIP download.

How do I add the favicon to my website?

Upload the downloaded files to your site, then paste the HTML link tags shown on this page into the head section of your pages.

What image should I start with?

A square image works best. Non-square images are automatically center-cropped to a square before resizing.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no limit, since all processing happens on your own device.
