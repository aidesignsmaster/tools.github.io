Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The video is played and re-recorded locally in your browser using the MediaRecorder API, and is never uploaded anywhere.

What format is the trimmed video saved as?

The downloaded clip is saved as a WebM file. Browsers cannot cut most video formats without re-encoding, so the output format is WebM regardless of what you uploaded.

Why does my browser say trimming is not supported?

This tool relies on browser features (captureStream and MediaRecorder) that are best supported in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers. Older browsers or some versions of Safari may not support it.

Will the trimmed video lose quality?

Because the browser re-encodes the selected section rather than cutting the file directly, there can be a small quality or file-size difference from the original.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark, since all processing happens on your own device.
