Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Working Tools Box ("we", "us", "our"), operated by Somnath Mahato from India, collects, uses and protects information when you visit workingtoolsbox.vercel.app (the "Site").

By using the Site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Your files stay with you

Our tools are built to run in your browser. When you open an image, PDF or video in one of our tools, it is processed on your own device. We do not upload your files to our servers and we cannot see them.

Some tools download code libraries or model files from content delivery networks (CDNs) such as cdnjs or jsDelivr so that they can work. These requests do not contain your files.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email address and message when you email us or contact us on social media.
  • Usage data: we use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited, how long people stay, device and browser type, the referring site and an approximate location based on IP address.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider (Vercel) may log technical details such as IP address, request time and browser information for security and performance.
  • Cookies: small files stored on your device. See our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain and improve the Site and its tools.
  • To understand which tools are popular and fix problems.
  • To reply to your messages and feedback.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent abuse.
  • To show ads in the future (see Advertising).
  • To follow the law when required.

Advertising

We may show ads on the Site in the future, including ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. To learn more, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Site, such as Google Analytics, Vercel (hosting) and, in the future, Google AdSense. They have their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Links to other sites

The Site links to other websites and platforms such as Facebook and YouTube. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

How long we keep information

Messages you send us are kept for as long as needed to reply and handle your request. Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics settings. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. Because your files are processed in your browser, they are not exposed to our servers. Still, no method of transmission or storage on the internet is 100% secure.

Your rights and choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing muma90748@gmail.com. You can also block cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

We aim to follow applicable laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and where relevant the GDPR and UK GDPR for visitors from those regions.

Children

The Site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Services such as Google and Vercel may process data in countries outside India. By using the Site you understand that your information may be handled in those countries.
