Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Merging and splitting both happen entirely in your browser, and your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Will my PDF's text still be selectable after merging or splitting?

Yes. Unlike tools that redraw pages as images, this tool copies the original PDF pages directly, so text stays fully selectable and searchable.

How do I specify which pages to extract when splitting?

Use a page range like 1-3,5,8-10 to extract those pages into one PDF, or choose Split Every Page for a ZIP with one PDF per page.

Can I reorder PDFs before merging?

Yes. Use the up and down arrows on each uploaded file to change the order before merging.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark, since all processing happens on your own device.
