Our story

Working Tools Box started in August 2026 with a simple idea: useful tools should be easy to find, quick to use and free for everyone.

It was built to help people with their daily work and to grow, step by step, into an independent business. Today you can find tools for images, PDFs and video, and many more tools and small games are on the way.
