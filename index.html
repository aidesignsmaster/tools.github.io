ABOUT THE TOOLS

Everyday File Jobs, Done in One Place

Working Tools Box brings the small jobs that come up every day into one tidy site. When a photo is too large to send, the image compressor and resizer make it lighter. When a form wants a PDF, the image to PDF tool turns your pictures into one file, and the PDF compressor, merger and editor help you finish the rest. Converters switch between JPG, PNG and WebP, and the sharpener and upscaler make small pictures larger and clearer.

For creative work there are tools to remove backgrounds, add watermarks, split a picture into a grid, recolor objects, build collages, make memes, pick colors and extract palettes. Video tools let you trim a clip, join several clips, shrink a large video, make a GIF or turn photos into an animated video with music. You can also read the text in a picture with OCR, generate QR codes for links, Wi-Fi and UPI payments, create favicons, compare two texts and make stylish text.

The tools are designed to work in your browser, so there is nothing to install, and they are made to be easy on phones as well as computers. Open any tool, read the short steps on its page and finish your task in a minute or two. If you want a break, the free games section has Ludo, Chess, Carrom and more.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the tools on Working Tools Box really free?

Yes. Every tool and game is free to use. There are no accounts, no payments and no watermarks added to your results.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

The tools are built to run in your browser, so your pictures, PDFs and videos are processed on your own device instead of being sent away for editing. A few features download small helper files the first time you use them, for example the language data for text recognition. Each tool page explains what it does.

Do I need to install anything or sign up?

No. Open a tool, add your file and download the result. Everything works in a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a phone, tablet or computer.

Which tools can I use for images?

You can compress, resize, crop, convert, sharpen and upscale images, remove backgrounds, add watermarks, split pictures into a grid, recolor objects, make memes and collages, pick colors, extract palettes, read EXIF details and extract text with OCR.

Can I work with PDF files and videos too?

Yes. You can turn images into a PDF, turn PDF pages into pictures, compress, merge, split and edit PDFs. For video you can trim, merge and compress clips, make GIFs, and turn photos into an animated video with music.

Why do some video tools take as long as the video?

Browsers cannot change most video formats directly, so the video tools play your clip and record the result in real time. A one-minute clip takes about a minute. Keep the tab open and in front until the progress bar finishes.

Is there a limit on file size or how many files I can use?

Limits depend on the tool and on your device. Many tools accept several files at once, for example up to 50 images in the sharpener and image to PDF tools. Very large files can need a lot of memory, so the page tells you if a file is too big.
