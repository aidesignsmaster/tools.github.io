Microworker Review: Tasks, Payouts and Tips

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: micro task platform with social, sign up and clipping tasks
  • Cost to join: free
  • Tasks we saw: $0.02 to $0.05, higher ones up to about $3
  • Minimum cash out: $5 in our check
  • Payout: to your own USDT or USDC wallet

Microworker (microworker.app) is a simple task platform where you complete small online jobs and get paid in cash. Most tasks take a minute or two, such as following an account, liking a post or registering on a website. This guide shows what the task list looks like, how the payout works and what to check before you start. It is based on our own account. Our balance is still $0.05, so we describe the cash out page but not the payout speed. The name is similar to other sites, so make sure you use the official address.

What is Microworker?

Companies post tasks, and workers do them for a small reward. The main menu has Tasks, My work and Wallet. The account page has Wallet, Activity, Payout method, Skills and assets, Invite friends and Settings. On the Tasks page you can filter by All, Microtasks and Clipping, and a live message shows when other workers got paid, which helps you see that tasks are being completed.

The tasks we saw

The list showed 45 tasks. These examples show the pay level:

  • Create an account on a website: $0.05.
  • Search, share and get a bonus on a listing site: $0.05.
  • Follow an account on Twitter, Instagram or LinkedIn: $0.02.
  • Like a post on Twitter: $0.02.

Each task shows how many people have finished it, how many are in progress and sometimes how many spots are left. Higher paying tasks also appear, up to about $3, but most simple tasks pay a few cents. A few tasks need a specific phone, and the page tells you when a task needs an iPhone or an Android phone.

Daily activity

The Tasks page shows a notice that says without a new task today, today will not count. This looks like a daily activity feature, so doing at least one task a day keeps your streak going. Open the "How it works" link on the site for the exact rules. You can also add your skills so the platform shows tasks that fit you, and turn on notifications or Telegram to hear about new tasks.

How the payout works

The Wallet page says you send the money you earned to your own USDT or USDC wallet. In our check the minimum cash out was $5, and a progress bar showed our balance of $0.05 out of $5.00 and said we needed $4.95 more. You need a crypto wallet that supports USDT or USDC, and you should set it up under Payout method.

Reality check: at $0.02 per task you need about 250 tasks to reach $5, and at $0.05 per task you need about 100. Higher paying tasks are the fastest way to reach the minimum.

How to get started

  1. Sign up for a free account on the official Microworker site.
  2. Add your skills under Skills and assets.
  3. Open Tasks and pick one with clear instructions.
  4. Complete it exactly as written and submit it.
  5. Check My work to see approved and pending tasks.
  6. Set up your wallet under Payout method and cash out at $5.

Tips to earn more

  • Choose higher paying tasks first, then fill the day with quick ones.
  • Do at least one task daily so the day counts.
  • Read each task fully before you start, because a missed step can mean no payment.
  • Turn on notifications so you do not miss new tasks.
  • Use a wallet and network you already know how to use.

Things to be careful about

  • Sign up tasks: some tasks ask you to register on another website. Only share details you are comfortable sharing.
  • Social accounts: follow and like tasks use your social profiles. Do not share passwords with anyone and follow the rules of each social site.
  • Crypto payments: check your wallet address and the network before you cash out, because crypto payments cannot be reversed.
  • Payments: never pay anyone to unlock tasks or speed up a payout.

What a typical session looks like

Because the tasks are short, you can do a few whenever you have a spare minute. Ten follow or like tasks at $0.02 to $0.05 each would add up to about $0.20 to $0.50, so reaching $5 takes many sessions. That is why it helps to look for the higher paying tasks and to check the list daily, since popular tasks fill up and show fewer spots left.

Is Microworker worth your time?

No platform can promise income, and we have not cashed out yet. Joining is free, and each task shows its price and how many people have finished it. Try it for a week, see how many approved tasks you get, and decide whether the $5 minimum and crypto payout suit you.

Who is Microworker good for?

Microworker suits people who like very short tasks, use social media and already have or are happy to set up a USDT or USDC wallet. It is not a good choice if you want a fast first payout or do not want to use crypto.

Our verdict

Microworker is a simple platform with clear task information and a transparent $5 cash out bar. The rewards are small and the payout is crypto only, so it works best as a slow side income. Start with the higher paying tasks and stay consistent.
