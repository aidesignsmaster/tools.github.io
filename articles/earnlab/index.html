EarnLab Review: Offer Walls, Surveys and Payouts

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: reward hub with offers, surveys and 19 partner walls
  • Cost to join: free
  • Balance shown in: US dollars
  • Withdraw: PayPal, crypto, gift cards and skins
  • Minimum we saw: $2 for USDC on Solana, with about $0.01 network fee

EarnLab is a free platform that pays you for completing offers, surveys and other tasks from many partner walls. You collect your balance in dollars and cash out through PayPal, crypto, gift cards and more. This guide explains the dashboard, the kinds of tasks, the withdrawal screen and the risks, based on our own account. We have not withdrawn yet, so we describe the withdrawal form but not the payout speed.

What is EarnLab?

EarnLab works like a hub. It lists featured tasks and surveys on its own pages and also gives you access to 19 offer, survey and watch walls from other companies. Your balance is shown in dollars, and a Wallet button at the top takes you to deposits and withdrawals. The bottom menu has Home, Tasks, My Tasks, Surveys and Menu, so it is easy to use on a phone. A live feed at the top shows recent completed tasks from other users, and a banner advertises a Monthly Race with a $2,500 prize pool. Read the race rules on the site before you count on it.

Types of tasks

Featured tasks

Featured tasks show the highest reward. On our screen they ranged from $0.15 to $239.90, for example Whale.io, Crypto.com, Clumsy Arrow, Slime Loop and Sky Plunger. The large amounts are totals for finishing many steps or reaching high levels, so they are not a quick payment. Small tasks like the $0.15 one are fast.

Surveys

Featured surveys show the time and the reward. We saw a Premium Survey of 6 minutes paying $1.68. EarnLab also lists survey walls such as Prime Surveys and Prime Insights.

The walls

The walls section holds the most tasks. The filter showed 19 walls, with 13 for offers, 5 for surveys and 1 for watching videos. Names include Torox, Prime Insights, Prime Earn, Adscend Media, AdToWall, RevU, myChips, MM Wall, Aye-T Studios, AdGate Media and Lootably. One wall, Hang My Ads, was locked until we earned $1.00 elsewhere.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official EarnLab site.
  2. Open Surveys and try a short one first.
  3. Browse Tasks and pick an offer with clear steps.
  4. Follow every step in order. You can see started offers under My Tasks.
  5. Open the Wallet and check the withdrawal options once you have a balance.

Withdrawals: what we saw

The Wallet window has three tabs: Deposit, Withdraw and Redeem Code. You can pick your country, and the list changes by country. On the screen we checked (India), the options were:

  • Cash: PayPal International.
  • Crypto: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Solana, Tether (USDT) and USD Coin (USDC).
  • Gift cards: Google Play.
  • Skins: Counter-Strike.

We opened the withdrawal form for USDC on the Solana network. It asks for the chain, your wallet address and the amount, and it has a Max Amount button. For this method the form said the amount must be at least $2, and it showed an estimated network fee of $0.01 and an estimate of what you would receive. Other methods can have different minimums and fees, so check the form for the method you want. A low fee like this makes the Solana network a good choice for small balances.

Honest note: our balance is still $0.00, so we have not requested a payout. The rule that your balance must be higher than the fee is shown on the form itself.

Tips to earn more

  • Start with surveys and simple offers to learn how rewards are credited.
  • Choose offers whose steps you can finish. A huge reward is worthless if you quit halfway.
  • Check the reward per minute before starting a survey.
  • Use only one account and answer honestly.
  • Pick the withdrawal method with the lowest fee for your balance.

Things to be careful about

  • Gambling bonuses: the Special section had a Gamdom option with a bonus. It is a gambling site, so it is optional and not suitable for everyone.
  • Offers that need spending: some of the biggest rewards, such as finance or game offers, can require deposits or in-app purchases. Read the requirements first and never spend money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Wrong network: sending crypto on the wrong chain or to the wrong address can lose it for good. Check both.
  • Personal data: share only what you are comfortable sharing with partner sites.

Is EarnLab worth your time?

No platform can promise income, and we have not withdrawn from EarnLab yet, so we cannot confirm payout speed. What we can say is that joining is free, the withdrawal form shows the minimum, the network fee and your estimated payout before you confirm, and every task shows its reward. Start with surveys and small offers, keep records of what you finish and test a small crypto withdrawal first.

Who is EarnLab good for?

EarnLab suits people who want many offer walls in one place and a withdrawal list with several crypto options. It is a fair choice for beginners who are comfortable using a wallet, but it is not a quick way to make money.

Our verdict

EarnLab has a modern design, 19 walls and a low minimum on at least one crypto method, with a $2 minimum and a $0.01 network fee for USDC on Solana. Be selective with high reward offers, check the minimum and fee of your chosen method, and avoid gambling related options.
