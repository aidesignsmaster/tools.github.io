Cointiply is a rewards website where you earn coins by completing offers, surveys, paid ads and daily missions, then cash out in cryptocurrency. It has been around for years and has a clear dashboard that shows how each way of earning works. This guide explains what you can do on Cointiply, how coins convert to money and how withdrawals work, based on our own use. We have received payouts from it.

What is Cointiply?

Cointiply pays you in coins. The dashboard shows your balance in coins and its value in dollars. On the screens we checked, 3,202 coins were shown as about $0.32, so 10,000 coins is about $1. Once your balance is high enough, you exchange coins for crypto and send it to your own wallet.

Ways to earn on Cointiply

Games and apps offers

This is the section with the highest upside. You install a game or app and reach milestones, such as a certain level, and each milestone pays a reward. For example, one game offer showed a total of up to $21.53, with small rewards at early levels and bigger ones at higher levels. The dashboard labels this category "up to $297" for the best offers. These offers take days or weeks, so they suit patient players.

Surveys

Surveys show the estimated time and reward before you begin, which helps you pick the ones worth your time. Rewards vary by survey, and you can be screened out if you do not match the target audience.

PTC ads

PTC means paid to click. You view an ad for a short time and get a small reward. These take under a minute and pay very little, but they are the easiest way to build a daily habit.

Missions, streaks and quests

Daily streaks, quests, spins and bonus missions give extra coins for coming back regularly. The dashboard also has a community chat where users share what is working.

What can you realistically expect?

Be ready for small steps at the start. In the game offer we looked at, the first milestones paid only $0.02, $0.07 and $0.16, while the full total of $21.53 needed you to keep playing up to a high level. That is typical: the early rewards are tiny, and the larger amounts come to people who stay with an offer for a long time. PTC ads and daily streaks add a few cents each day. If you mix surveys, one or two game offers and your daily missions, your balance can grow steadily, but it will not make you rich overnight.

How to get started

  1. Sign up for a free account on the official Cointiply site and verify your email.
  2. Open the dashboard and claim your daily streak reward.
  3. Try a few quick PTC ads and one survey to see how rewards are credited.
  4. Pick one game or app offer and follow the milestone list carefully.
  5. Check the cash out page when your balance grows.

How withdrawals work

The cash out page lets you withdraw in Bitcoin, Dash, Dogecoin or Litecoin. The minimum depends on the coin. On the Litecoin option we saw a minimum of 30,000 coins, about $3, and the page showed no fee. The Bitcoin option had a higher minimum, so check the cash out page for the current number for each coin.

To withdraw you enter the amount in coins, paste your wallet address and confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed up that way. The page warns that the wallet address cannot be changed after you submit, so double check every character. Network fees on your receiving side can still apply, and the value of crypto can go up or down between the time you withdraw and the time you sell.

Interest note: the withdrawal page says that if your balance falls below 35,000 coins, you will not earn the 5% interest. If you want to earn interest, read the terms on Cointiply and decide whether to keep coins in your account instead of withdrawing small amounts.

Tips to earn more

  • Log in every day. Streaks and missions reward regular visits.
  • Check offer details first. See how many steps there are and how long they take before you start.
  • Pick game offers that match what you already like to play.
  • Use your own wallet that you control, and test a small withdrawal first.
  • Keep one account only and use accurate information in surveys.

Things to be careful about

  • Sponsored gambling offers: the dashboard shows sponsored casino and betting ads. They are optional. Do not deposit money you cannot afford to lose, and make sure gambling is legal and allowed for your age where you live.
  • Crypto prices change: the dollar value you see can change by the time you sell.
  • Wallet mistakes: a wrong address can mean lost funds, and withdrawals cannot be reversed.
  • In-app purchases: you do not need to spend money to complete earning offers. Be wary of any offer that asks for payment.
  • Security: use a strong, unique password and turn on two-step login if it is available.
