RewardJoy Review: Offers, Surveys and Payouts

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: rewards site with game and app offers, surveys and partner offer walls
  • Cost to join: free
  • Minimum payout: $5 in our check
  • Payout options: PayPal, bank, crypto and gift cards
  • Fee we saw: PayPal 3.5% plus $0.30

RewardJoy is a free rewards website where you earn money by completing game and app offers, surveys and other tasks, then cash out through PayPal, a bank, crypto or gift cards. This guide explains how the dashboard works, what the rewards look like, what payout options exist and what to be careful about. It is based on our own account. We have not reached the minimum payout yet, so we describe the withdrawal screen but not the payout speed.

What is RewardJoy?

RewardJoy pays you when you complete tasks for advertisers and research companies. The menu has Dashboard, Offers, Surveys, Browsing, Ads, Affiliate and Challenges. The site supports several languages, including Hindi, and shows your balance in your local currency. The dashboard has three boxes: your main balance, your purchase balance and your referrals. There is also a level system that starts at Novice, Level 0.

Ways to earn on RewardJoy

Game offers

Game offers show a large reward, for example between ₹1,537 and ₹1,771 on the offers we saw. Treat these as the total you can reach, not a quick payment. In most game offers the reward is earned in stages as you reach levels, so the big amount can take days or weeks.

App offers

App offers are usually smaller and faster. We saw rewards of about ₹7.90, ₹10.20 and ₹17.30 for simple app tasks. Be careful with a few high paying app offers, such as casino and betting apps, which we explain below.

Offer partners

RewardJoy also gives access to partner offer walls such as Timewall, Torox, PubScale and Adscend Media. Each one has its own list of tasks, so you can compare and pick the easiest ones.

Surveys

Special surveys show the time and the reward before you start. On our dashboard we saw a 20 minute survey at ₹24.50 and a 10 minute survey at ₹24. Comparing the reward with the time, the shorter survey paid better per minute. Always check this before you begin.

Challenges

The Challenges page includes a daily challenge with a $100 reward pool for the top 100 users. It is a competition, so only the highest ranked users win.

How much do small offers pay?

The live feed of completed offers shows what other users received: many small offers paid about ₹0.85 to ₹1.13 each. This is a good reminder to set realistic expectations. Real money comes from the bigger multi-step offers and from being consistent.

How to get started

  1. Sign up for a free account on the official RewardJoy site and choose your language.
  2. Open Surveys and complete your profile questions.
  3. Try one or two quick app offers to see how rewards are credited.
  4. Pick one game offer and follow its steps carefully.
  5. Check your balance and open the cash out page once you get near the minimum.

Payouts and fees

The cash out page has two tabs: Cash and Gift Cards. Under cash you can choose local banks, PayPal or bank transfer. Under crypto you can choose USDT, Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC, Faucetpay, Litecoin, Dogecoin and Solana. In our check the minimum payout was $5, but confirm it on the withdrawal page because it can change.

Fees depend on the method. PayPal showed a fee of 3.5% plus $0.30. If that fee applies to a $5 withdrawal, you would receive about $4.52, so PayPal is not ideal for small amounts. Compare the fee shown for each method before you cash out.

Honest note: our balance is still below the minimum, so we have not yet withdrawn from RewardJoy. We will update this guide after our first payout.

Tips to earn more

  • Compare reward and time before you start a survey or offer.
  • Read every step of an offer. Missing one can mean no reward.
  • Give honest, consistent answers in surveys to avoid being screened out.
  • Use one account only.
  • Check the fee of each payout method and pick the cheapest one.

Things to be careful about

  • Casino and betting apps: some app offers, such as a casino app, pay a lot, but gambling is risky and may be restricted by law or age. They are optional. Never deposit money to earn a reward.
  • Staged rewards: big game offers may take a long time. Do not spend money to finish them.
  • Personal data: only share what you are comfortable sharing in surveys and sign ups.
  • Wallet addresses: double check the address and the network before you withdraw crypto, because crypto payments cannot be reversed.

Is RewardJoy worth your time?

No platform can promise income, and we have not yet received a payout from RewardJoy, so we cannot confirm payout speed. What we can say is that joining is free, the withdrawal methods and PayPal fee are shown openly, and the offers list the reward before you begin. Start small, keep a note of the offers you finish, and test one low-value withdrawal as soon as you reach $5 before you invest more time.

Who is RewardJoy good for?

RewardJoy suits people who like mobile games, surveys and comparing offers, and who are happy with slow, steady progress. It is not a fast way to make money.

Our verdict

RewardJoy has a clean dashboard, many languages and plenty of payout options, with a $5 minimum in our check. Be selective with offers, watch the fees, and avoid gambling related offers.
