TimeBucks Review: Tasks, Surveys and Payouts

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: rewards site with tasks, surveys, games, offerwalls, clicks and referrals
  • Cost to join: free
  • Minimum payout: $3 in our check
  • Game offers seen: about $7.70 to $57.04 in total rewards
  • Cash out: choose on the Redeem page

TimeBucks is a free rewards website with many ways to earn: tasks, surveys, game offers, daily streaks, partner offer walls, ad clicks and referrals. Its dashboard puts everything on one page and shows a progress bar toward your first cash out, which makes it easy to follow your balance. This guide explains the earning options, the payout minimum and the points to watch, based on our own account. We have not cashed out yet, so we describe the payout setup but not the payout speed.

What is TimeBucks?

TimeBucks pays you rewards for completing tasks for advertisers and research companies. The top of the dashboard shows four numbers: Rewards, Referral, Bonus and Total. A progress bar below them shows how close you are to a cash out. On our account the total was $0.587 and the bar showed that $2.41 more was needed, which matches a $3 minimum payout.

Ways to earn on TimeBucks

The main menu has nine earning options, so there is something for most people:

  • Tasks: small jobs such as sign ups and social actions.
  • Surveys: paid questionnaires from several survey providers.
  • Games: install a game and reach levels for bigger rewards.
  • Daily Streak: a reward for coming back every day.
  • Offerwalls: lists of offers from partner companies.
  • Clicks and Bonus Clicks: view ads for a small reward.
  • Hourly Crown: an extra reward linked to regular activity.
  • Refer: invite friends and earn from their activity.

Surveys

A banner at the top highlights a new survey, for example 15 minutes for $0.43. The list of recent survey completions by other users shows what to expect. Many payouts were small, about $0.09, while better ones reached $0.47, $0.69 and even $1.40. We saw survey providers such as Cint, Pure Spectrum, BitLabs, CPX Research and Dynata. As on every survey site, you can be screened out, so do not rely on a single survey.

Game offers

The Games page has Android and iOS filters, and offers show their total reward. On our screen the rewards ranged from $7.70 for Idle Soap ASMR to $57.04 for Crazy Aim, with many around $14 to $17. These totals are earned in stages as you reach levels, so they take days or weeks. Partners such as Torox, BitLabs, Prime Earn, Aye-T Studios, myChips and RevU have their own game lists, and Torox showed a +20% label.

Ladders and bonuses

TimeBucks runs ladders, which are competitions. The Games page advertises a $10,000 Gaming Ladder, and the top of the dashboard shows the best earners with bonuses between $4 and $30. Only the top users win these prizes, and they usually go to people who play a lot, so treat them as a bonus rather than a plan.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official TimeBucks site.
  2. Open Surveys and finish your profile so you get more matches.
  3. Claim your Daily Streak reward every day.
  4. Pick one game or offerwall offer and follow every step.
  5. Watch the cash out progress bar and open Redeem when you are close to $3.

Payouts

In our check the minimum payout was $3. The side menu has a Redeem page where you choose how to withdraw, so check it for the current options and any fees. At the bottom of the page you can see countdown timers for "invoice cut off" and "rewards are sent", which suggests payouts go out in cycles, so a request may not arrive instantly.

Read the fine print: the footer says that displayed balances and ad credits are platform records, not bank deposits or stored funds, and that TimeBucks is not a bank. Read the Terms of Use before you start so you know how rewards and credits work.

We have not reached the minimum yet, so we have not tested a payout. We will update this guide after our first one.

Tips to earn more

  • Log in daily for streaks and bonuses.
  • Choose surveys with a good reward for the time they take.
  • Pick game offers you actually enjoy playing.
  • Do not chase ladders if you only have a few minutes a day.
  • Keep one account only and use honest answers.

Things to be careful about

  • Never pay to earn: TimeBucks has an Advertise page for people who want to buy ads. You do not need to buy anything to earn.
  • Game offers: do not spend money on in-game purchases to finish an offer.
  • Referral promises: be careful with anyone who promises big referral income.
  • Personal data: only share what you are comfortable sharing in surveys and sign ups.

Is TimeBucks worth your time?

No site can promise income, and we have not cashed out yet, so we cannot confirm payout speed. What we can say is that joining is free, the minimum is low, and every offer shows its reward before you start. Begin with a few surveys and your daily streak, keep a note of what you finish, and test a $3 cash out before you invest more time.

Who is TimeBucks good for?

TimeBucks suits people who like variety and a low $3 payout limit, and who can check in every day. It is not a quick way to make money.

Our verdict

TimeBucks has many ways to earn, a clear cash out progress bar and a low minimum. Expect small rewards from most tasks, take the bigger game offers slowly, and read the terms about how rewards and credits work.

Ready to try it? Open the official site, read the rules and start with a small first task.
