JumpTask Review: How to Earn With Tasks and Withdraw

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: task platform with surveys, watch, social, game and passive income tasks
  • Cost to join: free
  • Reward unit: coins, where 1,000 coins equals $1 on the cards we checked
  • First withdrawal: $1. Later withdrawals: $0.50
  • Best for: people who like short tasks on a phone

JumpTask is a free task platform where you complete small online jobs and collect coins. Tasks include watching videos, following social accounts, answering surveys, trying games and even filming simple everyday activities. The rewards are small on their own, but they add up if you do tasks regularly. This guide explains what you will see inside, what the tasks pay, and how withdrawals work, based on our own use of the platform. We have received payouts from it.

What is JumpTask?

JumpTask works like a marketplace. Companies that want views, sign-ups, feedback or data pay JumpTask, and JumpTask passes part of that to people who do the work. You see every task as a card with a title, a short description and the reward in coins, with the dollar value shown beside it. On the task cards we checked, 1,000 coins equals $1.

The kinds of tasks you will find

The task list can be filtered by type, which makes it easy to find what suits you. These are the main groups we saw:

  • Watch to earn: watch a YouTube video or subscribe to a channel. These usually pay a few cents.
  • Search and follow: search for a profile on a site such as Instagram, TikTok or X and follow it. These are the quickest tasks and pay the least.
  • Social media tasks: join a community, follow a page or complete a small action on LinkedIn, X or Telegram.
  • Surveys and play to earn: answer surveys or try partner games and offers. These take longer but pay more.
  • Multi-task and multi-level tasks: one offer split into several steps, with a reward for each step.
  • Passive income: apps that earn in the background, such as bandwidth sharing apps.
  • POV tasks: short videos of your hands doing a chore, used to train AI, for example folding laundry or wiping a table.

There are also filters such as Most Popular, High Rewards, 3x Reward and Premium Quick, so you can sort the list by what matters to you.

How much can you earn?

Here is what we saw on the task cards, so you can set realistic expectations:

  • Follow and social tasks: about $0.01 to $0.02 each.
  • Watch and earn tasks: about $0.02 to $0.10 each.
  • POV filming tasks: about $0.20 to $0.40 each.
  • Surveys and partner offers: from about $0.15 up to $4.50 for some multi-step survey offers.

Some sections also show a "Finish all for" bonus, which tells you the total you can collect if you complete every started task in that group. Treat JumpTask as a way to earn small amounts of extra money, not a salary. Your results depend on your country, the tasks open to you and how much time you spend.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official JumpTask site and confirm your details.
  2. Open the task list and use the filters to pick a type, for example Watch & Profit.
  3. Tap a task and read the full instructions before you begin.
  4. Complete every step exactly as written and submit the proof if the task asks for it.
  5. Wait for the task to be checked. Your coins are added once it is approved.
  6. When your balance reaches the minimum, open the withdrawal page and request your payout.

Withdrawals: how much do you need?

This is one of the best parts of JumpTask. The first withdrawal needs only $1. After that, every later withdrawal needs just $0.50. A low limit means you can test the payout quickly instead of waiting weeks. The payout methods and any fees are shown on the withdrawal page and can change, so check them before you request your money.

Our advice: make your first withdrawal as soon as you reach $1. It is the simplest way to confirm that the payout works for you.

Tips to earn more and avoid rejections

  • Read each task fully. Most rejections happen because a step was skipped.
  • Start with multi-step offers that show a bigger total, but only if you have time to finish every step.
  • Keep one account only. Duplicate accounts can lead to a ban.
  • Give accurate information in surveys. Inconsistent answers often get you screened out.
  • JumpTask has announced VPN friendly tasks, but check the rules of each task before using a VPN.

Things to be careful about

  • Sign-up tasks: some tasks ask you to register on another website. Only share the details you are comfortable sharing.
  • Passive income apps: apps that share your internet connection use your data plan and have their own privacy terms. Read them first.
  • Prize draws: a task that says "Win an iPhone" is usually an entry, not a guaranteed prize.
  • Payments: never pay anyone to unlock tasks or speed up a payout.

Who is JumpTask good for?

JumpTask suits people who like short tasks on a phone and want a low payout limit. Students, stay-at-home parents and anyone with spare minutes can use it. It is not a good fit if you need a steady income or dislike tasks that involve signing up for other services.

Our verdict

JumpTask is a legitimate way to earn small amounts, with a very low first payout of $1 and later payouts from $0.50. Do not expect big money from the one-cent tasks, and focus on the better paying surveys and multi-step offers when they suit you.
