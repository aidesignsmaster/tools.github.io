Swagbucks Review: Surveys and $5 PayPal Payout

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: rewards site with surveys, cash back, partner offers and sweepstakes
  • Cost to join: free
  • Points: SB, where 100 SB is about $1
  • Lowest PayPal option: $5 for 530 SB in our check
  • Gift cards in India: Fastrack, Book My Show and Flipkart

Swagbucks is one of the best known reward sites. You earn points called SB by answering surveys, shopping through cash back deals, trying partner offers and claiming a daily bonus, then redeem them for gift cards or PayPal. This guide explains how Swagbucks works, what the surveys and offers look like, what you can redeem and what to watch out for. It is based on our own account, which is new and still at 0 SB, so we describe the redeem page but not the payout speed.

What is Swagbucks?

Swagbucks pays you when you complete activities for advertisers and research companies. The points are called SB, and on the redeem page 100 SB is worth about $1. According to the footer, the site is operated by Prodege, LLC. The top bar shows your balance, a Daily Bonus button and a search box. The footer lists the main ways to earn, Answer Surveys and Discover Deals, plus mobile apps and a browser extension.

First step: a banner asks you to verify your email address. Do this first, because it is needed to protect your account and receive rewards.

Surveys

The Answer Surveys page starts with a short 3 minute survey worth 5 SB, and says that finishing it unlocks more survey opportunities. After that the list shows a personalised mix. On our screen the surveys paid from 43 SB for 5 minutes up to 340 SB for 30 minutes, which is about $0.43 to $3.40. The page has tabs for Best Match, Short Surveys, Most SB, Max SB per Minute and Follow-Ups, so you can pick by time or by reward. Surveys have star ratings, and you can be screened out when you do not match what a company wants, so keep your profile honest and complete.

Discover deals and cash back

The Discover page lists popular stores with cash back. One example showed 1% cash back, or 1 SB for every $1 spent. This only makes sense if you were going to buy something anyway. Do not spend money just to earn points, because the reward is much smaller than the cost.

Partner offers

Under "Offers from our trusted partners", Swagbucks shows partners such as Tapjoy and RevU, each with rewards of up to 3,500 SB, about $35. These are totals for many steps, such as playing a game up to a high level, so they take time and are not a quick payment.

Daily Bonus and Swagstakes

The Daily Bonus button gives a small reward for coming back. Swagstakes is a sweepstakes where you enter for a chance to win. A sweepstakes is a chance, not income, so do not count on it.

What can you redeem?

The Redeem Rewards page changes by country. With India selected we saw:

  • Fastrack: ₹500 to ₹5,000 for 585 to 6,500 SB, with a sale of up to 10% off.
  • Book My Show: ₹500 for 650 SB.
  • Flipkart: ₹500 to ₹1,000 for 650 to 1,300 SB.
  • PayPal (US dollars): $5 to $100 for 530 to 10,030 SB.

Notice that PayPal costs a little more than the amount you receive: $5 costs 530 SB and $100 costs 10,030 SB, so about 30 SB seems to be added as a fee. You can filter rewards by card value and by the ones you can afford. In our check the lowest PayPal option was $5, which needs 530 SB.

Honest note: our account is still at 0 SB, so we have not redeemed anything yet. We will update this guide after our first redemption.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account and verify your email.
  2. Complete your profile so you get better survey matches.
  3. Take the 3 minute starter survey to unlock more.
  4. Claim your Daily Bonus every day.
  5. Try one partner offer only if you have time to finish all steps.
  6. Open Redeem Rewards when you have enough SB.

Tips to earn more

  • Check the Max SB per Minute tab to find the best paying surveys for your time.
  • Do not rush surveys, because careless answers can get you screened out.
  • Use the mobile apps or the browser extension if you shop online anyway.
  • Keep one account only.
  • Choose the reward with the lowest fee for the amount you redeem.

Things to be careful about

  • Spending to earn: cash back is small. Never buy something just for points.
  • Personal data: share only what you are comfortable sharing in surveys and offers.
  • Sweepstakes: they are chances to win, not earnings.
  • Look-alike sites: use the official swagbucks.com address and never pay anyone to boost your account.

Is Swagbucks legit?

No platform can be guaranteed, and we have not redeemed yet, so we cannot confirm payout speed from our own use. Joining is free, the reward prices and PayPal costs are shown before you redeem, and the site lists a company name and legal pages. Start with a few surveys and test one small redemption before you invest more time.

Who is Swagbucks good for?

Swagbucks suits people who like surveys, shop online and want gift card choices. It is a slow side income, not a quick way to earn.

Our verdict

Swagbucks has a clear starter survey, many ways to earn and a $5 PayPal option. Verify your email, keep your profile honest, avoid spending money for cash back and test a small redemption first.

Ready to try it? Open the official site, read the rules and start with a small first task.
