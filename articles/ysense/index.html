ySense is a paid survey site that also offers partner offers, games and a referral program. You earn dollars, which you can cash out through PayPal or gift cards. It is a good choice if you mostly want surveys with the reward and time clearly shown. This guide explains the surveys, what they pay per hour, the redeem options and what to watch out for. It is based on our own account, which has $0.19, so we describe the cash out page but not the payout speed.

What is ySense?

ySense pays you for surveys and offers from research companies and advertisers. The menu has Surveys, Offers, Play, Affiliates, Cashout, Forum and More. Your balance is shown in dollars, and the Redeem page shows how much you can redeem right now. The Forum lets members share tips, and the Affiliates section is for inviting others.

Surveys: the reward and the time

The big plus of ySense is that every survey shows the reward and the minutes before you start. These are the surveys we saw:

  • $0.43 for 12 minutes.
  • $0.66 for 15 minutes.
  • $0.89 for 17 minutes and $0.92 for 20 minutes.
  • $1.00 for 15 minutes and $1.12 for 15 minutes.
  • $1.20 for 15 minutes and $1.20 for 27 minutes.
  • $1.76 for 53 minutes and $2.11 for 40 minutes.

The page also says you can earn up to $2.11 in this list. If you work out the reward per hour, these surveys pay roughly $2 to $5 for each hour of survey time. The best ones were $1.00 and $1.12 for 15 minutes, which equals about $4 to $4.50 per hour. This is before you lose time to screen-outs, so real hourly earnings are usually lower.

Partner offers

Under "Offers from our trusted partners", ySense lists Tapjoy and RevU with up to $35 each, and Torox with up to $22.10. These totals need many steps, such as reaching a high level in a game, so they take days or weeks. The Play menu is another place for game offers.

PayPal costs $0.30 more than the amount you receive. In our check the lowest option was a $5 PayPal payout, which needs a balance of $5.30. You can filter rewards by card value and by what you can afford, and there is a Sweepstakes page too.

Honest note: our balance is $0.19, so we have not cashed out yet. We will update this guide after our first payout.

What a realistic first week could look like

If you finish five 15 minute surveys that pay about $1 each, you would earn roughly $5, which is the lowest cash out amount. That equals about 75 minutes of survey time. In practice you will be screened out of some surveys, so plan for more time than that, and see the first week as a way to learn which survey types match your profile. After a few days you will know which ones are worth your time.

How to get started

  1. Create a free ySense account and confirm your email.
  2. Fill in your profile completely so you get more survey matches.
  3. Open Surveys and choose ones with a good reward for the time.
  4. Try one partner offer only if you have time to finish every step.
  5. Check Redeem Rewards to see what you can afford.

Tips to earn more

  • Pick surveys with the best reward per minute, not just the highest total.
  • Answer honestly and consistently, because contradictions can get you screened out.
  • Do not start a survey if you are short on time, because leaving early earns nothing.
  • Use one account only.
  • Choose the cash out option that costs the least for your balance.

Things to be careful about

  • Screen-outs: you may spend minutes and still not qualify. This is normal on survey sites.
  • Personal data: only share what you are comfortable sharing.
  • High partner totals: they are long-term goals. Never spend money to finish an offer.
  • Scams: never pay anyone to boost your account or unlock surveys.

Is ySense legit?

No platform can be guaranteed, and we have not cashed out yet, so we cannot confirm payout speed. Joining is free, every survey shows its reward and time, and the cost of each redeem option is shown before you choose. Test one small $5 PayPal payout before you invest much time.

Who is ySense good for?

ySense suits people who prefer surveys over games and want to see the reward and the time clearly. It is a side income that builds slowly.

Our verdict

ySense shows survey rewards and minutes upfront, offers PayPal from $5 and has useful partner offers. Expect screen-outs, answer honestly and test a small payout first.
