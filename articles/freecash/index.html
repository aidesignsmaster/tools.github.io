What is Freecash?

Freecash works in the browser, and it also has a mobile app, which you can open with a button on the Rewards page. The bottom menu has four parts: Earn, My Offers, Cashout and Rewards. The top bar shows your cash balance and a second counter with a ticket icon, which was also 0 on our account. Check how the ticket counter works on the site before you plan around it.

The Rewards page

The Rewards page has three tabs: Quests, Bonuses and Invite Friends. When we opened Quests, the page said that no quests were available right now. Quests seem to appear and disappear, so check back from time to time. The Bonuses tab and the invite program are extras on top of your normal earnings.

My Offers

The My Offers tab keeps track of the offers you started. On our account it said that we had not started any offers yet. This is useful later, because you can return here to see which offers you are still working on.

Cash out options and minimums

The Cashout page shows your available balance, and two tabs: Cashout and My Withdrawals. Every method has a progress bar that shows how far your balance is from the minimum. This is what we saw:

  • PayPal: minimum $5.
  • Visa Prepaid: minimum $5.
  • Litecoin: minimum $0.50.
  • Dogecoin: minimum $0.50.
  • Amazon gift card and Bitcoin: shown as temporarily unavailable.

The page also had a small banner saying "Earn $2.50 to make your first cashout". This suggests a first cash out rule, so read the details on the site before you plan your first withdrawal. Methods can be unavailable for a while, as with Amazon and Bitcoin, so always check which options are open the day you want to withdraw.

Why the crypto minimum matters

A $0.50 minimum for Litecoin or Dogecoin is much lower than the $5 minimum for PayPal. If you already have a wallet, a crypto cash out lets you test the payout with a small amount. Network fees on your wallet side can still apply, so check the fee before you send, and double check your wallet address, because crypto payments cannot be reversed.

How does it compare with other sites?

Among the platforms we have covered, GemsLoot has a $0.50 minimum, Gain.gg starts at about $1, TimeBucks at $3 and ySense at a $5 PayPal payout. Freecash sits in the middle: very low for crypto, $5 for PayPal and Visa Prepaid. Which one is best for you depends on how you want to be paid, so it is worth trying two or three with small amounts.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official Freecash site.
  2. Open the Earn tab and look at the offers and tasks available for your country.
  3. Pick one offer, read all its steps and start it, so that it shows up under My Offers.
  4. Check the Cashout page to see which methods are available today.
  5. Reach the minimum for your chosen method and request your first payout.

Tips to earn more

  • Start with one offer at a time and finish it before you open another.
  • Check Quests and Bonuses regularly for extra rewards.
  • Test a small crypto payout first, if you use crypto.
  • Keep one account only and use honest information.
  • Take notes of what you finish, so you can spot offers that pay well for your time.

Things to be careful about

  • Offers that need spending: some offers ask for purchases or deposits. You never need to spend money to earn.
  • Gambling offers: if casino or betting offers appear, they are optional and may be restricted by law or age.
  • Temporarily unavailable methods: do not count on a single payout method.
  • Personal data: share only what you are comfortable sharing with partner sites.
