Idle-Empire Review: Surveys and Gift Card Rewards

By Working Tools Box • Updated November 2026

Earnings are never guaranteed and vary by country and effort. Rules, minimum payouts and available tasks can change, so check the official site before you start. Some links may be referral links, which can give us a small bonus at no cost to you.

Quick facts

  • Type: points site with surveys, offerwalls, videos and offers
  • Cost to join: free
  • Survey partners: TapResearch, CPX Research, BitLabs, PureSpectrum, TheoremReach, inBrain
  • Cheapest rewards: from 1,000 points
  • Rewards: gift cards and game credit, in two pages of choices

Idle-Empire is a points site where you earn points by answering surveys, trying offerwalls, watching videos and completing offers, and then spend them on gift cards and game credit. This guide explains the Earn and Withdraw pages, which survey partners it uses and what the rewards cost in points. It is based on our own look at the site. We have not withdrawn yet, so we describe what is shown, not the payout speed.

What is Idle-Empire?

The side menu has Earn, Profilers, Withdraw, Leaderboard, Community and Support. The Earn Points page has six sections:

  • Surveys: answer daily surveys from partner companies.
  • Offerwalls: offers from trusted partners.
  • Offers: curated offers chosen by the site.
  • Videos: watch videos for points.
  • Referrals: get your link and invite friends.
  • Promotions: special promotions from Idle-Empire.

Surveys and what they pay

The Surveys page gives three tips: answer honestly and consistently, expect to be rejected from some surveys, and check back often because new ones appear daily. It lists six survey partners, each with an estimated time and reward:

  • TapResearch: about 15 minutes, on average 5,000 points.
  • CPX Research: about 12 minutes, on average 4,700 points.
  • BitLabs: about 13 minutes, on average 5,200 points.
  • PureSpectrum: about 12 minutes, exactly 5,000 points per survey, and you can complete several per day.
  • TheoremReach: about 9 minutes, on average 3,800 points.
  • inBrain: about 14 minutes, on average 6,100 points.

TheoremReach is the shortest, and inBrain has the highest average. Compare the points with the time before you start, and remember that the average is not a promise for every survey.

What can you buy with points?

The Withdraw page is a catalogue of gift cards and game credit, each with a price in points. On page one we saw these starting prices:

  • Random Steam keys and KeyJoker balance: from 1,000 points.
  • Binance gift cards and ShadowPay: from 5,000 points.
  • Steam: from 22,000. iTunes: from 23,500. Amazon: from 42,000.
  • PlayStation Network: from 50,000. Riot Points: from 51,000. Xbox Live: from 52,500. Razer Gold: from 52,500.
  • Google Play: from 58,500. Eneba: from 61,000. Paysafecard: from 63,000. Kinguin: from 70,000.
  • Roblox: from 80,000. EA: from 130,000. Spotify: from 135,000. Netflix: from 193,200.

There are two pages of rewards. You can search, sort by popularity, and filter by country, category (skins, games, game points, entertainment, crypto, money, electronics, utilities), currency and region, including India. Availability can depend on your country, so set your country filter first.

How much is a point worth?

The list does not show a dollar value, so this is our own estimate: Binance gift cards start at 5,000 points, and a typical 12 to 15 minute survey pays about 5,000 points, so 5,000 points may be worth roughly $1. Please confirm this on the reward detail page before you plan, because we have not verified the exact rate. If the estimate is right, the lowest options at 1,000 points would be worth about $0.20.

A realistic example

If a 15 minute TapResearch survey pays about 5,000 points, four surveys in an hour would give about 20,000 points, which is close to the 22,000 points that a Steam gift card starts at. That means roughly an hour or a little more of survey time for your first Steam reward, before screen-outs. If our estimate of 5,000 points per $1 is right, that is about $4 per hour. Real results will be lower, because some surveys end early without a reward.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official Idle-Empire site.
  2. Complete your profile questions, so that surveys match you better.
  3. Open Earn, then Surveys, and try one partner at a time.
  4. Check the Withdraw page and set your country filter.
  5. Choose the lowest reward that you really want and work towards it.

Tips to earn more

  • Start with the shorter surveys to find out which partner suits you.
  • Check back every day, because new surveys are added daily.
  • With PureSpectrum, finish the survey until the "Thank you" page, otherwise it may not pay.
  • Choose a low-priced reward first, so you can test the whole process quickly.
  • Keep one account only and answer consistently.

Things to be careful about

  • Screen-outs: being rejected from some surveys is normal.
  • Region limits: some gift cards work only in certain countries. Do not use a VPN to change your country, because it can cause trouble with your points.
  • Personal data: only share what you are comfortable sharing with survey partners.
  • Offerwalls: do not spend money to finish an offer.

Is Idle-Empire legit?

No platform can be guaranteed, and we have not redeemed a reward yet, so we cannot confirm delivery time. The reward prices and survey partners are shown openly, and joining is free. Redeem a low-priced reward first, and then decide how much time to invest.

Who is Idle-Empire good for?

Idle-Empire suits people who like surveys and want gift cards or game credit rather than cash. It is a slow side income.

Our verdict

Idle-Empire has a large catalogue of gift cards, six survey partners and very low-priced starter rewards. Check the point value, set your country, and test one small redemption first.
