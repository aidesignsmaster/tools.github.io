Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

Your image is processed by an OCR engine running inside your own browser, not sent to a remote server for analysis. A one-time language data file is fetched from a content delivery network the first time you use a language.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, and several other common languages are available from the language dropdown.

Why is the extracted text inaccurate?

OCR accuracy depends heavily on image quality. Try a higher-resolution, well-lit, non-blurry image, straighten any tilted text, and make sure the correct language is selected.

Can this read handwriting?

This tool is optimized for printed text. Handwriting recognition is far less reliable and results may be poor.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no limit.
