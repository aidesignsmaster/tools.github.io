Play Checkers Online

Checkers, also called draughts, is one of the oldest and most popular strategy games in the world. This page lets you play checkers online in your browser, with no download and no sign-up. The board is drawn in 3D with solid pieces, smooth hops and a camera that turns to face the player whose turn it is. You can play against the computer on three levels or play with a friend on the same device.

The game uses the standard American rules on an 8 by 8 board. Each side has 12 pieces on the dark squares. Captures are mandatory, kings move one square at a time, and the game checks for draws by the 40 move rule and by repeated positions. The page shows every legal move with green squares, marks the last move in yellow, and shows when you must capture, so you can learn the rules while you play.

How to Play Checkers (Rules)

  1. 1

    Set up the board

    Each side starts with 12 pieces on the dark squares of an 8 by 8 board. Dark moves first and starts at the bottom. Light starts at the top.

  2. 2

    Move your pieces

    A normal piece, called a man, moves one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square. Tap a piece to select it, then tap one of the green squares to move.

  3. 3

    Capture by jumping

    To capture, jump over an enemy piece next to you and land on the empty square behind it. Captures are mandatory: if you can capture, you must. If the same piece can keep jumping, it must continue.

  4. 4

    Make a king

    When a man reaches the far row, it becomes a king. A king moves and captures one square at a time in any diagonal direction, forward or backward. If a man becomes a king in the middle of a jump, its turn ends there.

  5. 5

    Win the game

    You win when your opponent has no pieces left or has no legal move. A player who cannot move loses.

  6. 6

    Draws

    The game is a draw after 40 moves by each side with no capture and no man move, or when the same position happens three times.

Game Modes and Difficulty

Choose Play vs Computer to play one opponent. On Easy, the computer looks only one move ahead and often makes a weaker move on purpose, so a beginner can win. On Medium, it looks several moves ahead and makes only rare slips. On Hard, it searches deeper for about one second per move and never plays a random move. The computer never cheats. It follows the same rules, including mandatory captures, and it has no extra moves or hidden knowledge.

Choose 2 Players to play with a friend on the same device. The turn pill shows whose go it is, and the board turns to face each player. You can use the Flip view button at any time to turn the board around.

Checkers Tips and Strategy

Control the centre. Pieces in the middle of the board have more choices and are harder to trap. Try to keep your back row pieces at home as long as you can, because they stop the other side from making kings. Move your pieces forward in pairs so one piece can protect another, and avoid sending one lonely piece far ahead where it can be jumped.

A common mistake is forgetting that captures are mandatory. You can use this to your advantage. Offer one piece so the opponent must jump, then take two pieces in return. Before every move, check what the opponent is forced to do next. Checkers has been played for hundreds of years in many forms, and the American version on an 8 by 8 board has been studied so deeply that perfect play from both sides leads to a draw.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is checkers?

Checkers, also called draughts, is a strategy board game for two players. Each side has 12 pieces and tries to capture all the enemy pieces or leave the opponent with no legal move.

How do I play checkers here?

Tap one of your pieces, then tap a green square to move it. Pieces move diagonally forward. If you can capture, the page highlights the pieces that must capture.

What are the rules of checkers in this game?

This game uses American rules on an 8 by 8 board. Dark moves first. Captures are mandatory, multi-jumps must be finished, and kings move one square in any diagonal direction. You lose if you have no pieces or no legal move.

Is this checkers game free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no download, no sign-up and no payment.

Can I play checkers with a friend on one device?

Yes. Choose 2 Players and take turns on the same device. This is not online multiplayer. Both players use the same screen.

What do Easy, Medium and Hard mean?

Easy looks one move ahead and sometimes plays a weaker move. Medium looks several moves ahead with rare slips. Hard searches deeper and plays its best move. The computer follows the same rules as you.

Does checkers work on mobile?

Yes. The board fits phone screens and you play by tapping. It also works with a mouse, and you can move the cursor with the arrow keys and press Enter to select.

What is a good checkers strategy?

Hold the centre, keep your back row as long as possible, move pieces in supported groups, and think about what capture the opponent will be forced to make after your move.

Do I have to capture in checkers?

Yes. If any capture is available, you must make one. If the capturing piece can jump again, it must keep jumping. If a man reaches the king row during a jump, its turn ends.
