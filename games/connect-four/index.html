Play Connect Four Online

Connect Four is a simple game with surprisingly deep tactics. Two players take turns dropping coloured discs into a tall, upright board. Each disc falls to the lowest free space in the column you choose. The first player to line up four discs in a row wins the game. This page gives you a free 3D version that runs right in your browser. There is nothing to download and you do not need an account.

You can play with a friend on the same phone, tablet or computer and pass the device after each move, or you can play against the computer. The board is drawn in 3D, so you can drag sideways to look at it from a different angle. The drop keys above the board and the buttons on this page are 3D too. Everything works with a mouse, a finger or the keyboard.

How to Play Connect Four (Rules)

  1. 1

    The board and the discs

    The board has 7 columns and 6 rows, so it holds 42 discs. One player uses red discs and the other uses yellow discs. The players move one after the other, and Red always goes first.

  2. 2

    Drop a disc

    On your turn, click or tap a column, or press the 3D key above it. Your disc falls to the lowest empty space in that column. You cannot put a disc in a full column, and you cannot skip your turn.

  3. 3

    Make four in a row

    The first player to connect four of their own discs in a straight line wins. The line can run across, up and down, or diagonally in either direction. The winning discs glow and pop out of the board.

  4. 4

    A full board is a draw

    If all 42 spaces are filled and nobody has four in a row, the game is a draw. The game always ends, because every move fills one space.

  5. 5

    Who plays first

    Against the computer, you play Red and move first. The computer plays Yellow. With two players on one device, Player 1 is Red and Player 2 is Yellow.

Game Modes and Difficulty

Choose Play vs Computer to face an automatic opponent. On Easy, about half of the computer moves are random, so a beginner can win. On Medium, the computer looks about four moves ahead and notices most quick threats, but it still slips now and then. On Hard, the computer searches many moves ahead, up to about twelve in roughly one second, and it never plays a random move. The computer follows the same rules as you and cannot cheat.

Choose 2 Players to play with a friend on this device. You take turns, and the turn badge above the board shows whose turn it is. This is pass-and-play on one screen. It is not an online game, so both players need to be at the same device.

Connect Four Tips and Strategy

Start in the middle. The centre column is part of more possible lines than any other column, so discs there give you more ways to win. Try to build two threats at once. A double threat means you can win in two different places, and your opponent can only block one of them. Before every move, check what your opponent could win with their next disc, and also check the space above your own disc.

A common mistake is playing in a column and letting your opponent drop on top to complete a line. Another mistake is watching only the rows and forgetting the diagonals. Connect Four was sold as a board game from 1974, and computer scientists solved it in the late 1980s. They found that the first player can always force a win with perfect play, as long as the first disc goes in the centre column.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Connect Four?

Connect Four is a two-player strategy game. Players drop red or yellow discs into a board with 7 columns and 6 rows. The first player to make a line of four discs wins.

How do I play Connect Four on this page?

Choose a mode, then click or tap a column to drop your disc. You can also use the Tab key to reach a column and press Enter. Drag sideways on the board if you want to turn the 3D view.

What are the rules of Connect Four?

Players take turns, and Red goes first. A disc falls to the lowest empty space in the column you pick. Four discs in a row, across, up and down, or diagonal, wins. A full board with no winner is a draw.

Is this Connect Four game free?

Yes. It is free to play, it runs in your browser, and you do not need to download anything or sign up.

Can I play with a friend on one device?

Yes. Choose 2 Players and take turns on the same phone, tablet or computer. This page does not offer online play between two different devices.

What do Easy, Medium and Hard mean?

Easy makes many random moves and is good for beginners. Medium looks about four moves ahead and sometimes slips. Hard searches much deeper and does not make random mistakes. The computer never cheats on any level.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The board fits small screens, the buttons are large, and you can tap a column to play. On a phone you can swipe sideways on the board to turn the 3D view.

What is the best strategy for Connect Four?

Play in the centre column early, because it is part of the most possible lines. Then try to create two threats at once so your opponent cannot block both.

Who goes first, and can the game end in a draw?

Red always goes first. In vs Computer mode you are Red. A draw happens when all 42 spaces are filled and nobody has four in a row.
