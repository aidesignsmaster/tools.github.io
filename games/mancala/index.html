Play Mancala Online

Mancala is one of the oldest board games in the world, and it is still fun today. In the version on this page, called Kalah, two players sow stones around a board with twelve pits and two stores. The goal is simple: collect more stones in your store than your opponent. You can play Mancala online free here, with no download and no sign-up. The board is drawn in 3D, and it stays perfectly still while the stones move.

Choose Play vs Computer to practise alone, or choose 2 Players and share one screen with a friend. This page does not use online matchmaking. Both players take turns on the same phone, tablet or computer. There are no dice, so the result depends on your planning and not on luck. A full game usually takes five to ten minutes.

How to Play Mancala (Rules)

  1. 1

    The board

    Each player has six pits in a row and one big store on the right. Every pit starts with four stones. You play the bottom row, and your store is on the right. Your opponent plays the top row, and their store is on the left.

  2. 2

    Sowing stones

    On your turn, pick a pit on your side that has stones. Take all the stones and drop them one by one into the next pits, going counter-clockwise. You drop a stone in your own store when you pass it, but you skip your opponent's store.

  3. 3

    Extra turn

    If your last stone lands in your own store, you play again. You can win several extra turns in a row.

  4. 4

    Capture

    If your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, and the pit opposite has stones, you capture those stones and your last stone. They all go into your store. If the opposite pit is empty, the stone just stays where it is.

  5. 5

    End of the game

    The game ends when all six pits on one side are empty after a move. The other player puts the stones left on their own side into their store.

  6. 6

    Winning

    The player with more stones in their store wins. If both stores are equal, the game is a draw. Glowing pits show your legal moves, and a white ring marks the last pit played.

The computer has three levels, and none of them cheats. Easy plays a random pit about half of the time, and otherwise takes the pit that gives the biggest quick gain. It is a good start for beginners. Medium plans four moves ahead and takes extra turns and captures, but it makes a random move now and then. Hard plans many moves ahead, counts extra turns and stones on each side, and never makes a random move. Because Mancala has no luck, Hard is tough to beat, so look for long chains of extra turns.

Mancala Tips and Strategy

Extra turns are the heart of the game. A pit gives an extra turn when its stones equal the number of steps to your store. The pit next to your store needs one stone, the next one needs two, and so on. Start with the pit closest to your store, then follow with the next one, to chain several extra turns in one go. Keep an empty pit opposite a full pit of your opponent, because a last stone in that empty pit captures a big heap. Also check what your opponent can capture after your move.

Do not rush to empty your own side. When one side runs out, the other player keeps all stones still on their side, so stones on your side are worth something at the end. Common mistakes are leaving a big pit opposite an empty pit, and ignoring the opponent's extra turns. Mancala games have been played for thousands of years in Africa and Asia, and the word comes from an Arabic word that means to move. The Kalah rules used here became popular in the United States in the 1940s, after William Julius Champion Jr. promoted the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mancala?

Mancala is a family of ancient sowing games. This page uses Kalah, the most popular version. Each player has six pits and one store. You pick up stones from a pit and drop them one by one around the board. The player with the most stones in their store at the end wins.

How do I play Mancala online here?

Choose Play vs Computer or 2 Players. Click or tap one of your glowing pits. The stones are sown one by one to the right, into your store, and then around your opponent's side. Plan your moves to win extra turns and captures.

What are the rules of Mancala (Kalah)?

Each pit starts with four stones. Sow counter-clockwise, skip your opponent's store, and drop a stone in your own store as you pass it. A last stone in your store gives an extra turn. A last stone in an empty pit on your side captures the stones opposite. When one side is empty, the other player keeps the stones left on their side.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy picks a random pit half of the time and otherwise takes the biggest quick gain. Medium looks four moves ahead and sometimes slips. Hard looks many moves ahead and never plays a random move. Mancala has no dice, and the computer never cheats.

What is a good Mancala strategy?

Play pits that end in your store to earn extra turns, and start with the pit closest to your store. Keep an empty pit opposite a full pit of your opponent so you can capture. Also watch for the same chance on the other side.

What happens when one side runs out of stones?

The game ends at once. The other player moves all stones still on their own side into their own store. Then both stores are counted. The higher total wins, and equal totals are a draw.
