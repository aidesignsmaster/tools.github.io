Play Tic-Tac-Toe Online

Tic-Tac-Toe, also called Noughts and Crosses, is the first board game that many people learn. Here you can play Tic-Tac-Toe online for free, with no download and no sign-up. The board, the X and O pieces and even the buttons are drawn in 3D. Pieces drop onto the board with a soft landing, and a winning line lifts up so you can see it at once.

You can play with a friend on the same device, or play against the computer. The computer has three levels, Easy, Medium and Hard, so a young player can win and an expert can face a real challenge. Pick a mode and the game starts straight away. When a game ends, you can play again with one tap.

How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe (Rules)

  1. 1

    The board

    The board is a grid of 3 by 3 squares, nine squares in total. All squares start empty.

  2. 2

    Take turns

    X always goes first. In Play vs Computer you are X and the computer is O. In 2 Players mode, Player 1 is X and Player 2 is O. On your turn, tap one empty square to place your piece. A piece can never be moved or removed.

  3. 3

    Make three in a row

    The first player to get three of their own pieces in a straight line wins. A line can be across, up and down, or diagonal. There are eight possible lines. The winning line lifts up and turns green.

  4. 4

    A draw

    If all nine squares are full and nobody has three in a row, the game is a draw.

  5. 5

    Play again

    When the game ends, the Play Again button starts a new game with the same mode and level. The Home button takes you to the games page.

Game Modes and Difficulty

Play vs Computer has three levels. Easy plays random moves about half of the time, and it never blocks you, so it is a good level for beginners and young children. Medium always takes a win when it sees one and usually blocks yours, but it misses about one block in three, it does not plan ahead, and about one move in eight is a random slip. Hard plays perfectly. It checks every possible future and never loses. The best result you can get against Hard is a draw.

All levels follow the same rules as you. The computer only looks at the board, and it does not cheat. 2 Players mode is for two friends on the same device: pass the phone or share the screen, and take turns. It is not online multiplayer, and players do not connect from different devices.

Tic-Tac-Toe Tips and Strategy

Take the centre or a corner on your first move. The centre is part of four lines, a corner is part of three lines, and an edge square is part of only two. If the other player takes the centre, answer with a corner. If you go second and the other player starts in a corner, take the centre.

Try to build a fork. A fork is a move that creates two threats at the same time, so the other player can block only one of them. Before every move, check two things: can I win now, and must I block a win by the other player? Most lost games come from missing one of these two checks.

Common mistakes: playing on an edge square first, forgetting to block a diagonal, and trying to win too early instead of making a safe move. Against Hard, do not expect a win. With perfect play from both sides, Tic-Tac-Toe always ends in a draw, which is why it is called a solved game.

A little history: a very similar game, called terni lapilli, was played in ancient Rome. In the United Kingdom the game is named Noughts and Crosses. In 1952, a computer game called OXO ran on the EDSAC computer in Cambridge and let a person play Tic-Tac-Toe against a machine. If you count every possible order of moves, there are 255,168 different games of Tic-Tac-Toe.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tic-Tac-Toe?

Tic-Tac-Toe, also called Noughts and Crosses, is a two-player game on a 3 by 3 grid. One player uses X and the other uses O. The first player to make three in a row wins.

How do I play Tic-Tac-Toe online here?

Open the page, choose Play vs Computer or 2 Players, and the game starts. Tap an empty square to place your piece. X goes first.

What are the rules of Tic-Tac-Toe?

Players take turns placing one piece on an empty square. Three of your pieces in a row, in a column or on a diagonal, wins. If all nine squares are full and nobody has won, the game is a draw.

Is this Tic-Tac-Toe game free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no download, no account and no sign-up. It works in your browser.

Can I play with a friend on one device?

Yes. Choose 2 Players and take turns on the same screen. This is play on the same device. It is not online multiplayer.

What do the difficulty levels do?

Easy plays randomly about half of the time and never blocks. Medium takes wins and usually blocks your wins, but sometimes slips. Hard plays perfectly and never loses. The computer follows the same rules as you and does not cheat.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The 3D board scales to small screens, and every square is large enough to tap easily. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

What is a good strategy tip for Tic-Tac-Toe?

Start in the centre or in a corner. Before each move, check if you can win, and check if you must block. Try to make a fork, which is a move that creates two threats at once.

Can I beat the Hard computer?

No one can win against perfect play. The best result against Hard is a draw. If you make no mistakes, you will draw every game. Try Medium if you want to practise winning.
