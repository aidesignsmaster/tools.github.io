FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these games really free?

Yes. Every game on Working Tools Box is free to play. There are no accounts, no payments and no hidden fees.

Do I need to download anything or sign up?

No. The games run directly in your web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Just open a game and start playing.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, on the same device. Choose 2 Players (or 4 Players in games that support it) and take turns on one screen. Playing with friends on separate devices over the internet is not available.

How does the computer opponent work?

Choose Play vs Computer, then pick Easy, Medium or Hard. Easy makes mistakes, Medium is a fair challenge and Hard plays its best. The computer follows the same rules as you and never cheats.

Do the games work on mobile phones?

Yes. The games are designed for touch screens as well as mouse and keyboard, and they fit phones, tablets and desktops.

What happens when a game ends?

The winner gets a celebration screen. You can then choose Play Again to start a new game with the same settings, or Home to come back to the games list.
