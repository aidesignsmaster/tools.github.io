Play Reversi Online

Reversi is a classic strategy game for two players, and many people know it as Othello. Each player owns one side of a two-coloured disc, black or white. When you place a disc so that a line of your opponent's discs is trapped between your new disc and another one of yours, all of the trapped discs flip to your colour. The player with more discs when the game ends wins. This page offers a free 3D version that runs in your browser, with nothing to download and no account to create.

The board faces you almost straight on, so every square is easy to see and easy to tap. The discs are real 3D coins that flip over, and the squares lift like buttons when you point at them. Squares where you can legally play glow, so you never have to guess. You can play with a friend on the same phone, tablet or computer, or play against the computer.

How to Play Reversi (Rules)

  1. 1

    The board and the start

    The board has 8 rows and 8 columns. The game starts with four discs in the centre: two black and two white, placed diagonally. Black always moves first. Squares are named with a letter for the column and a number for the row, such as d3.

  2. 2

    Place a disc to flip

    On your turn you must place a disc on an empty square that flanks at least one line of opponent discs. A line can run across, up and down, or diagonally. Your new disc and another disc of yours must sit at both ends of that line. Every flanked disc flips to your colour.

  3. 3

    Only legal moves are allowed

    You can only play on a square that flips at least one disc. On this page those squares glow. If you pick any other square, nothing happens and you get a short message.

  4. 4

    Passing

    If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped and the page tells you so. The other player moves again. If neither player can move, the game ends. This can happen before the board is full.

  5. 5

    Winning

    When the game ends, the player with more discs on the board wins. If both players have the same number of discs, the game is a draw.

  6. 6

    Who plays first

    Against the computer, you play Black and move first, and the computer plays White. With two players on one device, Player 1 is Black and Player 2 is White.

Game Modes and Difficulty

Choose Play vs Computer to face an automatic opponent. On Easy, about half of the computer moves are random, so a beginner can win. On Medium, the computer looks about three moves ahead and values corners, but it still slips now and then. On Hard, the computer searches about six moves ahead and solves the last few moves of the game completely, and it never plays a random move. The computer follows the same rules as you and cannot cheat.

Choose 2 Players to play with a friend on this device. You take turns, and the turn badge above the board shows whose turn it is. This is pass-and-play on one screen. It is not an online game, so both players need to be at the same device.

Reversi Tips and Strategy

Corners are the most valuable squares, because a disc in a corner can never be flipped. Try to take corners, and avoid the squares right next to an empty corner, since they often hand the corner to your opponent. Mobility matters more than disc count in the early game. Having more legal moves than your opponent gives you choices, while having few moves can force you into a bad one.

A common mistake is grabbing as many discs as possible too early. Having many discs in the middle of the game usually gives your opponent more options. It is often better to flip fewer discs and keep your position compact. Reversi was first published in England in the 1880s, and the Othello version with a fixed starting position became popular in Japan in the 1970s.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Reversi?

Reversi is a two-player strategy game played on an 8 by 8 board. Players place black or white discs and flip the opponent's discs. The player with more discs at the end wins. Othello is a well-known version of the same game.

How do I play Reversi on this page?

Choose a mode, then click or tap a glowing square to place your disc. You can also use the arrow keys to move between squares and press Enter to play. Drag sideways on the board if you want to turn the 3D view.

What are the rules of Reversi?

Black moves first. You must place a disc that flanks at least one line of opponent discs, and those discs flip to your colour. If you cannot move, you pass. When nobody can move, the player with more discs wins.

Is this Reversi game free?

Yes. It is free to play, it runs in your browser, and you do not need to download anything or sign up.

Can I play with a friend on one device?

Yes. Choose 2 Players and take turns on the same phone, tablet or computer. This page does not offer online play between two different devices.

What do Easy, Medium and Hard mean?

Easy makes many random moves and is good for beginners. Medium looks about three moves ahead and sometimes slips. Hard searches deeper, solves the end of the game and does not make random mistakes. The computer never cheats on any level.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The board fits small screens and you can tap any glowing square to play. On a phone you can swipe sideways on the board to turn the 3D view.

What is the best strategy for Reversi?

Try to take the corners and avoid the squares next to an empty corner. Keep your options open by having more legal moves than your opponent, and do not rush to flip many discs early.

What happens if a player cannot move?

That player passes, and the other player moves again. The page shows a message when this happens. If neither player has a legal move, the game ends and the player with more discs wins.
