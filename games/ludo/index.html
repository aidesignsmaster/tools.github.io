Play Ludo Online

Ludo is a classic race game for two, three or four players. This version runs in your browser with no download and no sign-up. Pick Play vs Computer, 2, 3 or 4 Players, and the game starts straight away.

You roll one die each turn and move a token the number of steps shown. The dice are fair and the same for everyone, including the computer. The game shows which tokens can move, so it is easy to learn even if you have never played.

How to Play Ludo (Rules)

  1. 1

    Start

    Each player has four tokens in a base. Roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square. In the two-player game you sit on opposite sides of the board. Each color has its own Roll box on its side of the screen.

  2. 2

    Moving

    Move one token the full number rolled, clockwise around the board. After one lap your token turns into your colored home column. You must roll the exact number to land on the home triangle.

  3. 3

    Extra rolls

    You roll again after a 6, after capturing a token and after bringing a token home. If you roll three 6s in a row, the third roll is cancelled and your turn ends.

  4. 4

    Capturing

    If you land on a single opponent token, it goes back to its base. Tokens on a safe square cannot be captured. Safe squares are the four start squares and the four star squares.

  5. 5

    Winning

    The first player to bring all four tokens home wins. If you cannot move, your turn passes. If only one move is possible, the game plays it for you.

Game Modes and Difficulty

Choose Play vs Computer to play one opponent. Easy picks any legal move. Medium prefers captures, then leaving the base, then moving forward. Hard also weighs danger, safe squares and getting tokens home. The computer rolls the same dice as you and does not cheat. Choose 2, 3 or 4 Players to play with friends on one device. Everyone takes turns on the same screen. This is pass and play and not online multiplayer.

Ludo Tips and Strategy

Get tokens out of the base early, because you only get a start on a 6. Keep tokens on safe squares when an opponent is a few steps behind you. A token six or fewer squares in front of an enemy is in danger.

Do not rush one token alone. Moving a second token can set up a capture. Save an exact finishing roll in mind when a token is close to home. Ludo comes from the ancient Indian game Pachisi, which was played with cowrie shells.

What is Ludo?

Ludo is a race board game where each player moves four tokens around the board and into their home. The first to bring all four home wins.

What are the Ludo rules in this game?

You need a 6 to leave the base. A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll. Three 6s in a row cancel your turn. You need an exact roll to finish.

Is this Ludo game free?

Can I play Ludo with friends?

Yes, with 2, 3 or 4 players on the same device, taking turns. There is no online multiplayer.

What do Easy, Medium and Hard mean?

Easy chooses any legal move. Medium prefers captures and progress. Hard also considers danger, safe squares and finishing. The dice are fair for everyone.

Does Ludo work on mobile?

What is a good Ludo strategy?

Bring tokens out early, rest on safe squares when enemies are close behind, and spread your moves across tokens.
